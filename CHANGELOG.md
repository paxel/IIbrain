## [0.1.0]

### Added

- A Brain is a folder of Markdown files with their Attachments beside them; any folder of Markdown files opens as it is.
- Apps for Android, Linux, macOS and Windows that work on the same folder; a sync tool of your choice carries it between your devices.
- Capture typed text, files and links; a link is kept with the readable text of its page.
- On the phone, capture with the camera, by sharing from other apps, and by choosing IIbrain as the printer in any app.
- Text in photos, scanned pages and PDFs is read on the device and can be searched.
- New captures wait in the Inbox until you file them with a title, tags and a Folder.
- Full-text search over Notes and the text read out of their Attachments.
- Connect language models through an OpenAI-compatible endpoint and declare each as local or cloud.
- A connected model proposes title, tags and Folder for a new capture; nothing is filed until you confirm.
- Ask a question and get an answer made only from your own Notes, with the Notes it came from listed.
- Keep an answer as a Note, marked as written by a model and linked to its sources.
- Mark Notes and Folders as Private; what is Private reaches only a local model.
- On the phone, let a question draw on your calendar or contacts, read at that moment and never stored.
- When two devices changed the same Note, see both versions side by side and keep one or merge them.
- English and German.
