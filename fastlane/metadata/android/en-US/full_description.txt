IIbrain keeps what matters to you in one place and lets you ask it later.

Your Brain is a folder of Markdown files with their attachments beside them. Any other tool can read it. IIbrain never syncs it: a sync tool of your choice carries the folder between your phone and your computers.

Capture from anywhere: typed text, the camera, files, links, anything shared from another app, and Print Capture, which works even in apps that block copying and sharing: choose IIbrain as the printer. Text in photos, scanned pages and PDFs is read on the device and can be searched.

IIbrain brings no language model. Connect one of your own through an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, in the cloud or at home, and declare it local or cloud. A model proposes title, tags and folder for a new capture, and you confirm. Ask a question and get an answer made only from your own notes, with its sources listed.

Notes and folders marked Private reach only a model you declared local.

IIbrain asks for access to all files, because your Brain is a folder in the phone's storage that your sync tool also writes to. It uses the access for that folder only.

No account, no server. Free and open source.
